# Merfonte — first contact with a supplier

Send from **maris@merfonte.com**, never hello@. People answer people.
Keep it this short. Suppliers read the first four lines and decide.

---

**Subject:** Wholesale enquiry — Netherlands and Germany, launching 2026

---

Dear Bakel team,

I am opening **Merfonte** (merfonte.com), a curated online skincare store for
the Netherlands, Belgium and Germany, launching in 2026. The range is small on
purpose — face, eye and body care for customers over forty, chosen on
formulation rather than on marketing.

I would like to carry your line, and I am writing about wholesale terms.

To be clear about how I intend to work: **I am asking for a paid first order,
not consignment.** I would rather buy a small quantity outright and prove it
sells than ask you to carry the risk of a retailer you do not yet know.

Two questions before anything else, because the answers decide whether we fit:

1. For sales inside the EU, **who acts as the Responsible Person** for CPNP
   notification — do you retain that role, or does it pass to the importer?

2. Do you provide **ingredient concentrations for key actives**, and references
   to the studies behind your efficacy claims? I intend to publish both. I will
   not make a claim I cannot cite.

If this is of interest, I would be glad to receive your wholesale price list and
minimum order quantity.

Kind regards,

Māris Tuters
Merfonte — merfonte.com
maris@merfonte.com

---

## Why each part is there

**"a paid first order, not consignment"** — most enquiries a supplier receives
ask them to ship stock on trust. Saying this in the first email puts you above
that pile before they have read your terms.

**Question 1 decides the legal work.** If they keep the Responsible Person role,
your CPNP obligation is close to nil. If it passes to you, you need a PIF, a
safety assessor and a CPNP notification per product — real cost, real time.
This single answer changes your launch budget.

**Question 2 decides your positioning.** `DISCLOSED CONCENTRATIONS` and
`CITED, NOT CLAIMED` are on merfonte.com right now. A supplier who will not
give you the numbers makes those two lines undeliverable. A supplier who will
is worth more than one who is 15% cheaper — he is handing you the only thing
the large brands cannot copy.

## What not to say yet

- That the company is not registered. True, and you answer honestly if asked.
  Volunteering it in a first email only invites *"come back when you are set up."*
- The Estonia plan. Irrelevant until they want to invoice you.
- Any forecast of volume. You do not have one, and an invented number is the
  fastest way to lose a serious counterpart.

## If they ask for a VAT number

Answer plainly: registration is in progress, you expect it before the first
order, and you are happy to pay proforma in advance. Prepayment removes their
risk entirely and is the strongest thing an unknown buyer can offer.
