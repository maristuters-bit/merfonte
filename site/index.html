02 — The name

A spring
in the sea.

Off certain coastlines, fresh water rises through the sea floor — cold, clear, under pressure, surfacing in the middle of salt. Sailors once filled their barrels above them. In Italian, such a place is a fonte: a source.

We took the name because it describes a mechanism, not a mood.

03 — The position

Not
anti-aging.

Skin after forty is not a fault to be corrected. Barrier function changes, lipid production falls, renewal slows. These are measurable, and they respond to supply — not to persuasion.

We select against published evidence, say what is in the bottle and at what concentration, and make no claim we cannot cite.

For women and men. The biology is not that different.
